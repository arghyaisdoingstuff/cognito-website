The Fest

About Cognito

COGNITO is the department's international A-level intercollegiate Commerce fest that has advanced exponentially in caliber and scope, presenting young professionals with opportunities to explore and embrace the corporate arena. As a spearhead for corporate simulation, Cognito bridges the gap between imagined conditions and the true realities of business.

The Club

Professionals Festing Club

A student-led platform committed to cultivating competitive excellence, professional acumen, and leadership. Operating under the motto "Tempus • Imperium • Potentia", the Club enables students to apply classroom learning in high-pressure environments while developing sound judgment and strategic execution under real stakes.

The Department

Department of Professional Studies

Launched in 2002, the Department of Professional Studies (DPS) is built on a strong foundation of academic excellence, application-based learning, and futuristic vision. Home to more than 2,500 students, DPS offers an academic journey enriched by international professional body partnerships and seasoned corporate faculty.

The Institution

CHRIST (Deemed to be University)

Established in 1969, CHRIST (Deemed to be University) has undertaken path-breaking initiatives in Indian Higher Education. Dedicated to "Excellence and Service", the premier institution provides its students with comprehensive excellence, preparing them for leadership in an ever-evolving global landscape.
