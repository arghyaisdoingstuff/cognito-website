01 Magnetic Navigation Glider

Replaces static nav links with an intelligent, luminous gliding pill. The pill slides with spring damping to whichever link is hovered, expands dynamically to match word length, and snaps back to the active page when the cursor leaves.

TARGET: GLOBAL HEADER (ALL PAGES)
