Paradigm 01: The Monolithic Architectural Grid (Recommended)

Used by Linear, Stripe Press, and Teenage Engineering. Instead of individual cards floating in space, content sits in a continuous structural matrix separated by precision hairline rule lines with subtle coordinate markers. It feels engineered and authoritative, not like a template.
